Eight directions

Everything that walks is drawn facing one of eight screen directions: the four straight ones and the four diagonals between them. In the isometric view a step along a world axis is a screen diagonal and a step along a world diagonal is a straight screen direction, so the game really does walk all eight. The diagonal views of the pixel characters, ponies and creatures are built from the front, back and side pictures (TurnView in spriteRegistry.js); pictures you draw in editor.html fill the same eight slots.
