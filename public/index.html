WASD move (screen-relative) · always full speed (Dexterity and Animal Friendship let you get closer to animals) · hold E / Space or the left mouse button: use the tool in your hand where you point · right click: throw your lasso · click a villager to talk, an animal to feed or pet it · Menu > Controls: rebind keys · E: place a station (or hold the screen to aim, release to place) · R: rotate · L: throw your lasso (catch ponies; rarer ponies need a better lasso) · Z: get off your pony · U: let go of a roped animal (it asks first) · F: pick up / board · X: drop what is in your hand (Shift+X: the whole stack) · farming: a hoe tills grass or dirt, a watering can waters it each day, F with seeds plants (in their season), F at a ripe crop harvests · a brush grooms your pony · G: gear (lasso and bag slots) · V: emotes · 1-5 / wheel: tool belt · I: bag (your bag, your pony's pack, drop / destroy) · F at the General Store counter: shop · Q: craft · P: Pony Book · T: town (stockpiles, upgrades) · H / K / Y / O: your pony's powers while riding (rarity abilities, then the skills it learns as it levels: Pony Book) · riding one of your ponies, the bag (I) can make it your main pony (it follows you everywhere)
