How to
Pick a tile and paint with the mouse; Room draws walls round a floor in one drag (the last wall and floor you picked). The doormat is the way out: you arrive on the tile above it. Pick a piece of furniture and click to place it (drag a placed one to move it, right-click to remove). Walls at the top and left are drawn tall in the game, walls at the bottom and right low.
